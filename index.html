Why it exists

eezconvention covers the work that starts after a convention is announced.

The live app now supports the full convention lifecycle: admins configure conventions and venues, guests explore schedules and exhibitors, VIP teams track journeys, committees coordinate work, and organizers send updates while finance and operations keep execution moving.

Core Capabilities

A live convention control center for organizers, guests, and operations teams.

Conventions

Configure conventions, branding, and venues

Organizers can create branded conventions with logos, dates, sponsors, timezone-aware scheduling, venue links, and convention spaces that support the full event experience.

Guests

Deliver the full guest experience

Guests can enter the convention experience, browse live schedules, follow featured sessions, explore speakers, save favorites, review exhibitors, and stay on top of event updates.

Program

Publish sessions that guests can actually use

Program teams can manage sessions, speakers, tracks, spaces, visibility, and time-based discovery so the event schedule works both operationally and for guests on the floor.

Exhibits

Run an exhibitor directory with engagement

Exhibitors can be published into a searchable directory with categories, booth locations, bookmarks, likes, featured stalls, and detail views guests can return to later.

VIP

Track VIP journeys across committee handoffs

Transportation, hospitality, reception, and leadership teams can coordinate VIP arrivals, hotel handoffs, and status tracking from one shared journey workflow.

Operations

Keep committees aligned on work and spend

Committees can manage tasks, submitted requests, budget approvals, expenses, receipts, and operational ownership from a single convention workspace.

Broadcasts

Send live updates to the right audience

Organizers can send in-app broadcasts and push-style convention updates so guests and teams stay aligned on schedule changes, alerts, and critical event information.

Visibility

See convention health at a glance

Dashboards and notification views surface session activity, operational queues, unread updates, and convention status so leaders can respond before issues escalate.

Guest Experience

The guest flow is live and central to the product.

The app now includes a dedicated guest experience. Users can enter a convention as guests and move through guest-first tabs and discovery tools instead of being dropped into organizer operations.

That means the platform now handles both sides of the event: organizer execution and the live on-the-ground experience for convention guests.

A1

Enter the guest experience

Choose a convention and continue into the guest side of the app with guest-specific navigation.

A2

Follow the live schedule

Browse published program sessions, open session details, and keep up with what is happening next.

A3

Save favorites and discover exhibitors

Bookmark exhibits, revisit favorite booths, and explore speakers and event highlights from one flow.

A4

Stay updated during the event

Receive event updates and notifications so guests can react to schedule and convention changes in real time.
