EezConvention Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EezPlatforms LLC (“EezPlatforms,” “Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use EezConvention, including our mobile applications, website, and related services (collectively, the “Platform”).

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should not use the Platform.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to users of EezConvention, including convention organizers, administrators, committee members, volunteers, vendors, speakers, sponsors, guests, and other participants who access or use the Platform.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, services, venues, vendors, organizations, or platforms that we do not own or control, even if they are linked to or referenced from the Platform.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information depending on how you use the Platform.

Account Information

  • Name;
  • Email address;
  • Profile image, if provided through your authentication provider or uploaded by you;
  • Convention, committee, role, and membership affiliations;
  • Account preferences and notification settings.

Authentication Information

When you sign in using third-party providers, such as Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we may receive information authorized by you or provided by those services, including:

  • OAuth or account identifiers;
  • Email address;
  • Name or display name;
  • Profile image, if available;
  • Apple private relay email address, if you choose to use one.

Convention and Committee Information

  • Convention names, descriptions, schedules, locations, venues, and room assignments;
  • Committee names, memberships, roles, and responsibilities;
  • Task assignments, due dates, status updates, comments, and activity history;
  • Announcements, messages, collaboration records, and administrative actions.

Uploaded Content

  • Documents;
  • Images;
  • Schedules;
  • Messages and comments;
  • Vendor, venue, speaker, sponsor, or event-related information;
  • Other content submitted, uploaded, or shared through the Platform.

Usage Data

  • App interactions and feature usage;
  • Pages, screens, or features accessed;
  • Activity logs and audit records;
  • Error reports and diagnostic information;
  • Date, time, and frequency of Platform usage.

Device and Technical Information

  • Device type and model;
  • Operating system and app version;
  • Browser type, where applicable;
  • IP address;
  • Mobile device identifiers or push notification tokens;
  • Network and diagnostic information.

Support and Communications Information

  • Support requests;
  • Email communications with us;
  • Feedback, questions, or reports submitted to us;
  • Information you provide when requesting assistance.

Offline Subscription and Customer Information

EezConvention does not currently process online payments, in-app purchases, digital subscriptions, ticket sales, escrow transactions, payment card processing, or consumer payment transactions through the mobile application or website.

If an organization subscribes to the Platform through offline, invoice-based, proposal-based, contract-based, order form, statement of work, or other direct commercial arrangements, we may collect business contact, billing contact, organization, invoice, subscription status, and account administration information needed to manage that offline relationship. We do not collect or store payment card information through the Platform.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Platform;
  • Create and manage user accounts;
  • Authenticate users through Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or other supported providers;
  • Provide convention, committee, scheduling, task, messaging, and document-sharing features;
  • Manage role-based access permissions;
  • Deliver push notifications, service emails, announcements, and administrative communications;
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries;
  • Monitor security, detect fraud, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical issues;
  • Analyze usage trends and improve product functionality;
  • Administer offline customer, subscription, invoice, and commercial relationships;
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4. Convention Administrator Access and Organizational Visibility

EezConvention is designed for organizations and conventions that may use administrators, committees, roles, and permissions to manage access to convention-related information.

Depending on your role and the permissions configured by a convention administrator, certain information you provide may be visible to other authorized users within the same convention, committee, or organization. This may include your name, email address, profile details, committee membership, assigned tasks, messages, comments, uploaded files, and activity related to convention operations.

Convention administrators may manage user access, committee membership, roles, permissions, schedules, documents, messages, and other convention-related records. EezPlatforms LLC is not responsible for how a convention or organization chooses to configure internal access permissions, provided such configuration is supported by the Platform.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, analytics tools, and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain sessions;
  • Remember preferences;
  • Improve user experience;
  • Understand usage trends;
  • Diagnose technical issues;
  • Protect Platform security.

You may manage cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not function properly if cookies or similar technologies are disabled.

6. Analytics and Diagnostics

We may collect analytics and diagnostic information to understand how users interact with the Platform, identify errors, improve performance, and prioritize product improvements.

Analytics data may include device information, app version, feature usage, crash logs, screen interactions, and general usage trends. We do not use analytics to sell personal information.

7. Push Notifications

The Platform may send push notifications related to:

  • Tasks;
  • Announcements;
  • Convention updates;
  • Committee activity;
  • Messages and collaboration updates;
  • Administrative and security notices.

You may disable push notifications through your device settings. Some service-related or administrative communications may still be sent by email or through the Platform where necessary.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information and do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Within conventions and organizations: Information may be shared with authorized convention administrators, committee members, or other users based on roles and permissions.
  • With service providers: We may share information with vendors that help us operate the Platform, such as cloud hosting, authentication, analytics, support, communication, and notification providers.
  • With authentication providers: Information may be exchanged with Google, Apple, or other supported providers to authenticate your account.
  • For legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, court order, governmental request, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, security, users, or the Platform.
  • Business transfers: Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction involving EezPlatforms LLC.
  • With your direction or consent: We may share information when you direct us to do so or consent to the sharing.
